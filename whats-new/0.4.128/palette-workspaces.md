kind: feature
area: app
highlight: true
steps:
  - open: palette
    target: palette-workspaces
    caption: Every open project's workspaces, with the ones whose crew needs you first. Type part of a branch to find one.

---
⌘K now has a **Workspaces** group: one row per workspace across all your open projects, with its project and branch. Workspaces waiting on you come first, then working ones, then the most recent. Picking one switches to it like a click in the rail.
