---
title: Crews with emoji like ⚠️ import again
kind: fix
area: app
---
A crew whose names, prompts or stage instructions hold an emoji such as ⚠️, ✔️, ❤️ or 👩‍💻 now imports, on your Mac or a teammate's, instead of being refused for a hidden character. Stray or repeated selectors and joiners are still blocked.
