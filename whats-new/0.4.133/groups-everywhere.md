    target: palette-input
    caption: ⌘K finds your rail groups by name, and a group's name finds its missions.
    action: type

  - target: palette-groups
    caption: Choose a group to unfold it in the rail and put the focus on its header.
---
⌘-click or ⇧-click missions in the rail, then right-click for **Group N missions…**. On a focused mission or project, **⌘⌥G** moves it to a group, and ⇧F10 opens a mission's menu. Mission Control and archived missions now show the group too.
