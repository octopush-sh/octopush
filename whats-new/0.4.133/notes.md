## Octopush 0.4.133

### Features

- **Find groups in ⌘K, and group several missions at once** — ⌘-click or ⇧-click missions in the rail, then right-click for **Group N missions…**. On a focused mission or project, **⌘⌥G** moves it to a group, and ⇧F10 opens a mission's menu. Mission Control and archived missions now show the group too.

### Fixes

- **Dragging projects between groups says what it does** — A keyboard drag onto another group's project now shows the group about to take it last, as the drop does, and the **Into** / **Out of** cue stays right when the rail reloads mid-drag. Dropping a project on its own group's name does nothing, and the rail no longer takes the focus back long after a **Move to group**.
