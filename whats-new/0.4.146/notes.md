## Octopush 0.4.146

### Improvements

- **Your messages stand apart in a long Talk thread** — Your messages now sit on their own panel with the time you sent them, so a long Talk thread is easy to scan. The model's notes and tool calls hang from one line as quiet rows, and a round of more than five calls folds into one row you can open.

### Fixes

- **The run track shows where its hidden stages are** — When a Direct run has more stages than fit, the track now fades the edge that hides stages and puts **Earlier stages** at its left edge and **Later stages** at its right. A stage scrolled out of view no longer leaves a line leading in from nothing.
