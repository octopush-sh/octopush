## Octopush 0.4.105

### Features

- **A terminal beside the conversation in Talk** — In Talk, **⌘D** or the terminal icon puts one of your Run sessions beside the conversation: the same shell, still running. Run's panes and Talk's seat now come back as you left them after a restart.

### Improvements

- **Code reads apart at a glance** — The editor and the Review diff now give types, function names, strings, numbers and constants, and annotations or macros their own colours. Comments are quieter than code, and strings no longer look like comments. Go, Kotlin and Shell now colour keywords, calls, strings and constants.

### Fixes

- **Effort reaches Haiku 5.5** — An effort level set on a Haiku 5.5 stage or sub-agent now reaches the API. Before, it was silently dropped and the model's default applied.
