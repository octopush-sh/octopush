---
title: A terminal beside the conversation in Talk
kind: feature
area: app
steps:
  - open: mode/talk
    target: talk-seat-toggle
    caption: Open one of your Run sessions beside the conversation. ⌘D does the same.
    action: none
---
In Talk, **⌘D** or the terminal icon puts one of your Run sessions beside the conversation: the same shell, still running. Run's panes and Talk's seat now come back as you left them after a restart.
