---
title: Fast-mode turns in Run are priced at the fast-mode rate
kind: fix
area: app
---
Claude Code fast-mode turns on Opus 5 and Opus 4.8 now cost $10/$50 per million tokens in Usage,
twice the standard rate. Before, they showed at the standard rate, about half their real cost.
Fast mode on any other model shows as **unpriced**.
