## Octopush 0.4.104

### Features

- **Three and four sessions side by side in Run** — Run now shows up to **four** sessions at once: three put a large pane beside two smaller ones, four make a 2×2 grid. Panes stack on their own when side by side would leave one under 72 columns; with two or three panes, each pane's menu sets **Stack**, **Side by side** or **Automatic** for the workspace.

### Improvements

- **Choose the session beside with ⌘D** — **⌘D** in Run asks which session sits beside the current one, and **⌥⏎** in the ⌘⌥K switcher opens the selected session beside instead of switching. With every session already on screen you get an empty seat to open a new one in; closing a session on screen just closes its pane.
