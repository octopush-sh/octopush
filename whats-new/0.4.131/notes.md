## Octopush 0.4.131

### Features

- **Group a project's missions in the rail** — Right-click a mission and choose **Move to group › New group…** to file related missions under one foldable, optionally tinted header; dragging a mission onto a header adds it too. A group keeps its place among the other missions and stays when it is emptied, until you remove it.
- **Run's Companion shows context, changes and the mission** — In Run, the Companion now shows what the terminals can't: the focused Claude Code session's context and cost, the files your sessions changed, and the mission's worked time and spend. Context needs Claude Code's hooks: it shows in worktrees Octopush created, with the hooks setting on, and not for Codex or other CLIs. It replaces the session inspector.
