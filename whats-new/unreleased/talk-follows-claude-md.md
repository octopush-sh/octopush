Talk and its sub-agents now follow your `CLAUDE.md` files and the project's, with their imports, or
`AGENTS.md` when there is no `CLAUDE.md`. A project file that points outside the project is never
read. Edits apply on the next turn, and the Companion's **follows** row lists the files.
