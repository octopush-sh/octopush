The Direct launcher now shows a cost range, such as **est. $0.40–$1.10 · saves ~62% vs all-premium**.
It comes from this Mac's recent completed runs of each role, priced at the models you chose.
With little history the range is marked "rough", and a model with no price shows as unpriced, never $0.
