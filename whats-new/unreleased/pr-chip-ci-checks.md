title: The PR chip shows your branch's CI checks
kind: feature
area: app
steps:
  - target: pr-chip-checks
    caption: Sage when checks pass, amber while they run, rouge when one fails. Hover to see which.
    action: hover
---
The PR chip now shows the branch's CI checks, with the failing ones named in its tooltip. When a check fails, **Fix failing checks…** opens the Direct launcher with a brief naming the failures and the tail of their logs. You pick the crew and begin.
