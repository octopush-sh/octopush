GPT-6.1 Terra cache writes are now priced at $1.25 per million tokens, OpenAI's rate for the default 30-minute retention, and Terra can take images. Cache-write tokens reported by OpenAI and by Codex stages are now billed at the cache-write rate instead of as plain input, so run budgets match the bill.
