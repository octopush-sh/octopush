kind: feature
area: app
scenario: default
steps:
  - target: fleet-chip
    caption: The count of crews waiting on you now shows on the Dock icon too, and clears at zero.

The Dock icon now shows how many crews need you. Closing the window hides it, so Routines and
running crews keep going. Click the Dock icon to bring it back, and press **⌘Q** to quit.
