title: The welcome screen's main action leads again
kind: fix
area: app
---
"Set a crew on it" now keeps its framed primary look in every state, even before you type a brief. Its lettering turns full brass once a brief and a key are in place, and stays softer, though still clickable, while a key is missing. The quieter routes beneath it share one ink and one underline, and turn grey while they're unavailable.
