Settings › Usage now shows its totals, mode cards and spend rows as quiet placeholders while a large ledger is read, and the figures fade into the same places. A change of period keeps the previous figures until the new ones arrive, and a small "reading the ledger…" note sits in the filter bar meanwhile.
