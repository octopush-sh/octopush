The editor now keeps a trail of your jumps: go to definition, ⌘P, go to line and search results. Press **⌃−** (Ctrl+Alt+− on Windows and Linux) to go back, with the selection and scroll restored, and **⌃⇧−** to go forward. **⌥F7** / **⇧⌥F7** step through the changes in a file, and typing in large files is lighter.
