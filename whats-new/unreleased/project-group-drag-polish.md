---
title: Dragging projects between groups says what it does
kind: fix
area: app
---
A keyboard drag onto another group's project now shows the group about to take it last, as the drop does, and the **Into** / **Out of** cue stays right when the rail reloads mid-drag. Dropping a project on its own group's name does nothing, and the rail no longer takes the focus back long after a **Move to group**.
