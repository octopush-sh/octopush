title: Move a Fable 5.1 crew to Fable 5.2 in one click
kind: improvement
area: app
steps:
  - open: mode/direct
    target: pipeline-model-superseded
    caption: This crew still runs Fable 5.1. Fable 5.2 costs about 20% less per token. Switch it, or keep 5.1.
---
When a crew still uses Fable 5.1, as a stage model or an escalation model, the Direct launcher offers
**Switch this crew**: it moves that crew to Fable 5.2, which costs about 20% less per token. **Keep 5.1**
hides the offer for that crew. Nothing switches unless you ask.
