---
title: A mission can start after another one
kind: feature
area: app
steps:
  - target: rail-prerequisite
    caption: The hourglass says what this mission waits for. Hover it to read the wait.
  - target: mission-control-card-prerequisite
    caption: Mission Control shows the same wait on the mission's card.
---
Choose **Start after…** in a mission's menu to make it wait for another mission's run to pass its
last gate, or for its pull request to merge. Then its brief and crew wait in Direct for **Begin the
run**, unless you turned on **Start the run on its own**.
