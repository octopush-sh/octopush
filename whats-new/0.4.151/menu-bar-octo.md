---
title: The Octo in the menu bar counts crews that need you
kind: feature
area: app
scenario: default
steps:
  - open: settings/general
    target: settings-menu-bar
    caption: The Octo in the menu bar lists the crews waiting on you. Turn it off here.
    action: none
---
The Octo now sits in the macOS menu bar with the number of crews that need you. Its menu lists
them, the longest-waiting first, and opens the one you choose, even with the window closed.
