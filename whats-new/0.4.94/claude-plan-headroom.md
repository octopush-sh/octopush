    target: usage-claude-plan
    caption: Usage lists every plan window with its reset time and how old the reading is.

On a Claude Pro or Max plan, Claude Code stages now report your plan's 5-hour and weekly windows. The Companion shows them during a run, with a calm note near the limit, and Settings › Usage lists each one, when it resets and how old the reading is.
